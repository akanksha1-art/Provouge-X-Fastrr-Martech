Before The Numbers

First, what 0% MDR means, so the commercials read clearly

Payment cost is the part of checkout pricing that is easiest to misread. Here is the difference, in three steps.

STEP 1

MDR is the fee taken from every payment

The merchant discount rate is the percentage a payment gateway charges on each transaction. In a typical setup it is deducted from the brand's settlement before the money arrives.

STEP 2

With 0% MDR, it stays out of your settlement

fastrr works with the major gateways. The gateway's charge is deducted from a prepaid fastrr wallet instead of from Provogue's settlement, so the full payment amount lands in your account.

STEP 3

So settlement is cleaner, and cash arrives whole

The brand sees the full order value settle, which helps working capital and makes reconciliation simpler. The commercials page then shows fastrr's rate mode by mode.
